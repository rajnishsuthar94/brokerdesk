Import leads from Excel

1. Prepare your file

Your sheet needs at least Name and Phone columns. Optional columns: Email, Source, Budget Min/Max (e.g. 75 L, 1.2 Cr), Location, BHK, Property Type, Sale or Rent, Status, Priority, Next Follow-up (DD/MM/YYYY), Assigned To (team member's email) and Notes.

Excel (.xlsx) and CSV files both work. Downloads from property portals (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing…) can usually be uploaded as they are. Only the first sheet is read.
